# 💫 About Me:
Bernabé Sánchez Sos earned his bachelor's degree in Multimedia Engineering and his master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Alicante in 2023 and 2024, respectively. Driven by a strong interest in applying advanced technologies to real-world health challenges, he is currently pursuing a PhD in the Department of Computer Technology and Computing at the University of Alicante. His research focuses on the development of intelligent systems for healthcare applications. He is also an active contributor to the Tech4Diet-Person project, where he collaborates within a multidisciplinary team on the creation of a multimodal system designed to support personalized strategies for the treatment of obesity.
